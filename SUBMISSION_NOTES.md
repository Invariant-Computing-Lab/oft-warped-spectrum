# Submission Notes

## Current status
Submission-preparation package. The manuscript has not been peer reviewed and does not claim empirical validation of OFT.

## Recommended article positioning
Theoretical/computational physics or mathematical-physics paper focused on invariant reduction, numerical falsification, warped spectral reconstruction, and evidence boundaries.

## Core contribution
1. Reformulates the supplied OFT model under a transformation quotient rather than treating raw coefficients/labels as invariants.
2. Falsifies entrywise overlap and degenerate diagonal-Higgs quantities as fundamental invariants.
3. Independently reconstructs the finite-warp odd-profile spectrum with a second-order finite-difference generalized eigenproblem.
4. Maps the validity domain of the large-warp Bessel approximation.
5. Corrects a numerical inconsistency in the source Table A.1.
6. Shows that pure successive KK-root spacing cannot reproduce charged-lepton mass ratios over the tested bulk-mass branch.

## Claims that should remain excluded from abstract/title
- proof that OFT is a correct theory of Standard Model flavor;
- proof that electron, muon, tau are the first three KK modes;
- dark-matter identification of higher modes;
- collider or clinical/experimental validation;
- completeness of the finite invariant fingerprint for the continuum theory.
